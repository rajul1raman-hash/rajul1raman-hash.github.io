Where do outpatient no-shows come from?

A SQL analysis of real outpatient appointments: the no-show rate rises from 4.6% (same day) to 33.0% (booked 31+ days ahead), and bookings made 8+ days ahead drive 57% of all no-shows. Includes the SMS confounder, charts and tested recommendations.
